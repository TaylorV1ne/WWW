A complimentary seven-day home experience

Meet Wanda.

Wanda is a K8 water ioniser that connects temporarily to a suitable kitchen tap, filtering your water and producing a choice of waters including fresh, hydrogen-rich drinking water.

For seven days, Wanda lives in your kitchen so you can experience the water in everyday life. There's nothing to purchase and no obligation - simply a chance to discover how it fits into your home.

How it works

1. Send a short enquiry - tell me your name, area and the best way to reach you.

2. We check the details - I confirm availability, dates and that your tap will work.

3. Delivery & setup - I bring Wanda to you and show you how to use her.

5. Collection - I collect Wanda, no questions asked, no pressure to continue.

Home experiences are currently available within approximately one hour of Bedford, subject to availability and a suitable kitchen tap.

The honest answers

Questions people ask

Is there any charge for the seven days?

No. The Wanda home experience is complimentary and includes delivery, installation, guidance, support and collection. There is no obligation to purchase afterwards.

Will Wanda fit my kitchen tap?

Wanda connects to many standard kitchen taps, but not all. Before confirming, I'll ask for a photo of your tap so I can check.

Am I expected to buy afterwards?

Not at all. The experience is simply time to use the water and decide for yourself. There's no expectation or pressure to continue.

What happens after the seven days?

I collect Wanda at a convenient time. You can ask any questions or explore options, or simply conclude the experience there.
